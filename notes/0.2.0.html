Speedrun English 0.2.0

  • NEW Lessons with your AI agent · a new LESSONS tab asks your AI agent for lessons (translate, writing, reading, new knowledge, dictation), several kinds at once with a length for each, and copies a prompt that names this copy of the app. Your data moves to this version, backed up first.
  • NEW Do a lesson · work through a lesson on its own page, one block at a time in FOCUS, and submit it once every block has an answer. Each answer comes back marked correct, almost or wrong with feedback, and DO IT AGAIN starts the lesson over.
  • NEW Hold the Line · a second game: an airship drops supply crates carrying meanings, and you shoot each one down by typing its English word. It has its own sound effects and music.
  • NEW Key clicks switch · Settings has a KEY CLICKS switch that turns off a game's typing sounds.
  • IMPROVED ACTIVITY and Decks · ACTIVITY shows a deck being built and every change to your library, its DECK menu groups decks, a click on a Dashboard deck opens it in Decks, and ⌫ deletes a custom deck.
  • FIXED Preview and new word sets · Preview no longer changes a card's state, and NEW WORD SET draws a new set at random instead of swapping between the same two.
  • FIXED Small windows and clean screens · tabs, Setup, Settings tiles and card lists fit narrow windows without cutting words, and Results and Letter Rise no longer show doubled or garbled numbers.
